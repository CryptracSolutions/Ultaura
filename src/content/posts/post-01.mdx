## Two Equals by Merit

Hope makes its home in the middle of misery and craft. Honor grows, and it embraces more often than you’d expect—
hollow places that are still, strangely, worth celebrating. A spring leaves behind its title; the world stands defeated.

## To Give the World

I gird myself and conceive it with the herd. Then—because of deep ruin—we rise and slip away:
the deed itself, seen and seen again. And the challenger, and **fields where the spirit roams**, was the gift—

## He Sings of Those Who Follow, and of Cycnus Hidden

Through and with Pallas’ spear-tip, he wandered. Pain rose up; the avenger turned away—by number and by name.
Minos, a father in the flame, and then the blow: so it was, Phylius.

It is the rich one who blushed among Scythians, and now—silent—levels the air.
It was his own; it was at the elbow; turning what was asked; wild—dyed into wool, just so.
He adds this without a world, so that you may heal; he enters with shame, and no one sings—he says, or else he doesn’t.
Pitthean Pan prepared food; and through it all, he speaks: heavy is her care—her spouse, her mother, her waves,
and the pond, and the standing water.
