Getting started with Ultaura is quick and easy. In this guide, you'll create your account and learn what to expect from your free trial.

## Creating Your Account

1. Visit the [sign-up page](/auth/sign-up)
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a password
4. Verify your email
5. You're in!

## Your Free Trial

Every new account starts with a free trial so you can experience Ultaura before committing.

**What's included:**

- Duration: 14 days
- Call minutes: Up to 20 minutes per day during the trial
- Phone lines: 1
- All features: Yes

**Cancel anytime.** Your trial starts immediately and gives you full access to all features.

**No surprises.** Your trial ends after 14 days. Your card is collected at signup, and you are only billed if you keep the plan active after the trial.

## Choosing a Plan

After your trial, you can continue with a paid plan.

**Plan snapshot:**

- Care: $19 monthly or $180 annual, 200 minutes, 1 line
- Comfort: $49 monthly or $470 annual, 600 minutes, 2 lines
- Family: $99 monthly or $950 annual, 1,200 minutes, 4 lines
- Usage Based: $0 monthly, 0 minutes, unlimited lines

Save 20% with annual billing. [View full pricing details](/pricing).

## Your Dashboard

After signing in, you'll see your dashboard. This is your home base for:

- Managing phone lines for your loved ones
- Setting up call schedules
- Creating reminders
- Viewing insights and reports
- Managing your account and billing

Don't worry if it looks empty at first. In the next section, we'll add your first phone line.
