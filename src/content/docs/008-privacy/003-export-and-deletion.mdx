You can request a data export or delete data at any time.

## Data Export

A data export includes call history, insights, and memories. The export is delivered through a secure download. You will receive an email when it is ready.

### How to Request an Export

1. Go to the Privacy Center
2. Click "Request Data Export"
3. Confirm your request
4. Watch for the email with your secure download

## Privacy Data Deletion

You can delete privacy data from the Privacy Center. This removes stored insights, memories, and recordings tied to your account. Call metadata and reminders are preserved.

### How to Delete Privacy Data

1. Go to the Privacy Center
2. Click "Delete Privacy Data"
3. Confirm deletion

## Full Account Deletion

You can also delete your entire account. This removes all lines, schedules, and history. It cannot be undone.

### How to Delete Your Account

1. Go to **Settings → Profile**
2. Click **Delete Account**
3. Confirm deletion

Calls stop immediately. Data is removed within 30 days.
