Ultaura collects only what it needs to support your loved one and keep you informed.

## What Data Is Collected

- Call metadata (time and duration)
- Conversation insights (mood and topics)
- Memory summaries
- Safety events

## Call Recordings

Recordings are optional. They require explicit consent and can be turned off at any time. When enabled, recordings are stored in encrypted form.

## Data Retention Options

You can choose how long data is kept:

- 30 days
- 90 days
- 365 days
- Indefinite

## Security and Privacy

All data is encrypted. Ultaura uses industry-standard security practices. Your data is never sold.

## Changing Retention Settings

Go to the Privacy Center to update retention for your account and your loved one.
