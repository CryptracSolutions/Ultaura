Milestones help Ultaura celebrate important dates with your loved one.

## Examples of Milestones

- Birthdays
- Anniversaries
- Memorials
- Other meaningful dates

## Add a Milestone

1. Go to **Lines** and select your loved one.
2. Open **Milestones**.
3. Click **Add Milestone**.
4. Enter a title, type, and date.
5. Choose one-time or recurring.
6. Save.

Milestone dates use your loved one’s time zone.

You can edit or delete milestones at any time.
