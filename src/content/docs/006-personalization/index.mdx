Personalization helps Ultaura feel more familiar to your loved one. These settings shape how calls sound and what Ultaura talks about.

You can add milestones, adjust accessibility, and guide conversation topics. All settings are per line.

In this section, you’ll learn how to:

- Add birthdays, anniversaries, and other milestones
- Set hearing and cognitive support preferences
- Choose topics to enjoy and topics to avoid
