Accessibility settings help Ultaura speak in a way that feels comfortable for your loved one.

Go to **Lines → [Line Name] → Settings → Accessibility**.

## Hearing Mode

- **Normal**: Standard voice delivery
- **Enhanced clarity**: Clearer speech and enunciation
- **Slow pace**: Slower speaking speed

## Cognitive Mode

- **Normal**: Standard conversation style
- **Supportive**: More gentle prompts and reminders
- **High support**: Extra guidance and repetition

## Advanced Controls

- **Speech rate**: Fine-tune the pace. 1.0 is normal.
- **Context window**: How many recent calls Ultaura can reference.

These settings apply to this line only. They do not change phone volume.
