Conversation topics help Ultaura start friendly, familiar chats.

## Update Conversation Topics

1. Go to **Lines** and select your loved one.
2. Find **Conversation topics**.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Choose topics they enjoy.
5. Add any custom topics.
6. Add topics to avoid.
7. Save changes.

You can save up to five topics they enjoy.

Topics to avoid are respected. Your loved one can always steer the conversation during a call.

## How Topics Show in Insights

Topics appear in insights only with these sharing tiers:

- Tier 1: Basic Updates & Safety
- Tier 2: Wellness Check
- Tier 3: Full Summary
- Tier 4: Complete Visibility

Topics are shown at Tier 3 or Tier 4. Learn more in [Sharing with Family](/docs/insights-and-reports/sharing-with-family).

If you share insights with family, you can invite up to five recipients.
