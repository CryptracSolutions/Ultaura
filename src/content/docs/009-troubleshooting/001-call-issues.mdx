If calls are not connecting, start with these quick checks. Most issues are related to schedule settings or phone access.

## Call Not Connecting

Check the following:

- The line is verified
- It is not during quiet hours
- Vacation mode is not active
- The schedule time is correct for your loved one’s timezone

## Call Going to Voicemail

- Review the voicemail behavior setting
- Consider a different call time
- Ask your loved one to keep the phone nearby

## Call Quality Problems

Call quality depends on the phone line:

- Landlines are usually clear and stable
- Cell phone signal strength matters
- Try a different room or location

## Missed Calls

Ultaura retries twice within 30 minutes by default. Check **Call History** to see all attempts. Adjust your schedule if needed.

## Loved One Wants to Stop Calls

Your loved one can opt out at any time:

- Press 9 during a call
- Or simply ask Ultaura to stop
