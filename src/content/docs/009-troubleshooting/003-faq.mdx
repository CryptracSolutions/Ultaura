Here are quick answers to common questions. If you do not see your issue, reach out anytime.

## Getting Started

**How do I know if a call went through?**  
Check **Call History** on the line page. You’ll see each attempt and outcome.

**Can my loved one call Ultaura back?**  
Yes, if **Inbound Calling** is enabled in Line Settings.

**What if my loved one doesn’t want to talk?**  
They can hang up at any time. The next call will follow the schedule.

**How can my loved one stop calls entirely?**  
They can press 9 during a call or simply ask Ultaura to stop.

## Calls and Scheduling

**How long do calls usually last?**  
Calls can be as long as your loved one wants. They can hang up anytime.

**Can I change the call schedule?**  
Yes. You can edit schedules on the line’s **Schedule** page.

**What happens if my loved one hangs up early?**  
The call ends. Future calls continue as scheduled.

**Do reminders follow the call schedule?**  
No. Reminders are scheduled separately and are delivered at the time you set.

## Privacy and Sharing

**Can my loved one change what’s shared with me?**  
Yes. They can change sharing during a call.

**Is the conversation recorded?**  
Recording is optional and requires consent. You can manage it in the Privacy Center.

**Who else can see the insights?**  
Only people you invite in **Family Recipients**, and only at the sharing level your loved one allows.

## Billing

**What happens when I run out of minutes?**  
On a free trial, calls stop when minutes run out. On paid plans, you can upgrade or allow overages with a spending cap.

**Can I add more minutes mid-cycle?**  
Yes. You can upgrade your plan or allow overage minutes with a spending cap.

**Where do I update payment methods or view billing history?**  
Go to [Subscription Settings](/dashboard/settings/subscription).

**How do I cancel?**  
You can cancel from [Subscription Settings](/dashboard/settings/subscription).

## Technical

**Does my loved one need a smartphone?**  
No. Ultaura works on any phone, including landlines.

**Does Ultaura work internationally?**  
Not yet. Ultaura is currently available in the United States only.
