You can manage billing at [Subscription Settings](/dashboard/settings/subscription). This is where you update payment methods and view billing history.

## Upgrading your plan

Upgrades give you more minutes or more lines for your loved one. Changes are prorated so you only pay the difference for the rest of the cycle.

## Adding minutes mid-cycle

If you need more minutes before the cycle ends, you can upgrade your plan. You can also allow overages with a spending cap.

## Downgrading your plan

Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. You may lose access to extra lines if your new plan includes fewer lines.

## Switching to annual billing

Annual billing saves 20% on Care, Comfort, and Family plans.

## Overage cap

Set a spending cap to limit overage costs. You can change it anytime.

## Canceling your subscription

If you cancel, access continues until the end of the billing period. You can keep or change your data retention settings in the Privacy Center.

## Payment methods and billing history

Go to [Subscription Settings](/dashboard/settings/subscription) to update your card and view past invoices.
