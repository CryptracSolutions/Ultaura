You can track every minute used for your loved one. Open Dashboard → Usage to see your current balance and history.

## What's tracked

- Minutes used in the current billing cycle
- Minutes remaining
- Overage minutes and cost (if any)
- Call history with dates and duration

## Minutes pool

Minutes are pooled at the account level. All lines share the same pool.

## Usage alerts

Ultaura warns you when minutes are low. You will see alerts at 15 minutes remaining and again at 5 minutes remaining.

## Billing cycle

Your usage resets each billing cycle. You can see the next reset date in the Usage dashboard.

## Spending cap

A spending cap limits overage costs. When the cap is reached, calls stop until the next cycle.

Cap options:
- No limit
- $10
- $25
- $50
- $100

Overages only apply if you are not on the Free Trial.
