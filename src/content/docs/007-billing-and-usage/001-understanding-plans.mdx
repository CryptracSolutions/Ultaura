Ultaura offers flexible plans for your loved one or the whole family. All plans include the full experience and safety features.

## Plan comparison

- **Free Trial:** $0, up to 20 minutes per day for 14 days, 1 line, up to 5 reminders per line
- **Care:** $19 monthly or $180 annual, 200 minutes, 1 line, up to 5 reminders per line
- **Comfort:** $49 monthly or $470 annual, 600 minutes, 2 lines, up to 10 reminders per line
- **Family:** $99 monthly or $950 annual, 1,200 minutes, 4 lines, unlimited reminders
- **Usage Based (Pay As You Go):** $0 monthly, 0 included minutes at $0.15 per minute, unlimited lines, unlimited reminders

## Annual savings

Save 20% with annual billing on Care, Comfort, and Family plans.

## Free Trial details

Your Free Trial lasts 14 days. It includes up to 20 minutes per day, 1 line, and up to 5 reminders per line. Calls stop when the trial ends unless you upgrade. There are no surprise charges.

## Usage Based (Pay As You Go)

This plan has no monthly fee and no included minutes. You pay $0.15 per minute used. It includes unlimited lines and unlimited reminders. It is best for occasional use.

## Overages

Care, Comfort, and Family include a minutes pool. If you go over, extra minutes cost $0.15 per minute. Overages never apply on the Free Trial.

## Choosing the right plan

- Care: One line for your loved one and regular check-ins
- Comfort: Two loved ones, or longer calls
- Family: Multiple loved ones and shared minutes
- Usage Based: Pay only for what you use
