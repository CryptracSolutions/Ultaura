Ultaura offers flexible plans for your loved one or the whole family. All plans include the full experience and safety features.

## Plan comparison

- **Free Trial:** $0, 20 minutes over 3 days, 1 line, up to 3 reminders per line
- **Care:** $39 monthly or $399 annual, 300 minutes, 1 line, up to 3 reminders per line
- **Comfort:** $99 monthly or $999 annual, 900 minutes, 2 lines, up to 10 reminders per line
- **Family:** $199 monthly or $1,999 annual, 2,200 minutes, 4 lines, unlimited reminders
- **Usage Based (Pay As You Go):** $0 monthly, 0 included minutes at $0.15 per minute, 4 lines, unlimited reminders

## Annual savings

Save 15% with annual billing on Care, Comfort, and Family plans.

## Free Trial details

Your Free Trial lasts 3 days. It includes 20 minutes, 1 line, and up to 3 reminders per line. Calls stop when the trial ends unless you upgrade. There are no surprise charges.

## Usage Based (Pay As You Go)

This plan has no monthly fee and no included minutes. You pay $0.15 per minute used. It includes up to 4 lines and unlimited reminders. It is best for occasional use.

## Overages

Care, Comfort, and Family include a minutes pool. If you go over, extra minutes cost $0.15 per minute. Overages never apply on the Free Trial.

## Choosing the right plan

- Care: One line for your loved one and regular check-ins
- Comfort: Two loved ones, or longer calls
- Family: Multiple loved ones and shared minutes
- Usage Based: Pay only for what you use
