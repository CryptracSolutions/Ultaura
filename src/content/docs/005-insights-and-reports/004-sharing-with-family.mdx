You can share insights with other family members. Sharing is always based on your loved one’s consent.

## Enable Sharing First

Go to the **Privacy Center** and turn on **Family Sharing**. You must enable this before inviting anyone.

## Add Family Recipients

1. Go to **Privacy Center → Family Recipients**
2. Click **Invite Recipient**
3. Enter their name and email
4. Send the invite

You can add up to 5 recipients.

## Sharing Tiers

- **Tier 1:** Safety alerts and call stats
- **Tier 2:** Adds mood trends
- **Tier 3:** Adds conversation topics
- **Tier 4:** Adds concerns and relationships

## How Preferences Are Set

Sharing preferences are captured during calls. Your loved one can choose or change their level at any time.

## Request a Change

If you need an update, request a sharing change in the Privacy Center. Ultaura will ask your loved one on the next call.
