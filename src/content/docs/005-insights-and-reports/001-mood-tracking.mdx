Mood tracking shows how your loved one has been feeling across recent calls. It helps you spot changes and trends.

## What Mood Tracking Shows

- A simple mood trend over time
- Changes compared to recent calls
- A gentle signal, not a diagnosis

## Where to Find It

You can see mood trends in the **Insights** area for each line and on the main Insights dashboard.

## How It Works

Ultaura looks at tone and language during calls. It compares recent calls to build a simple trend.

## Using Mood Trends

Look for steady changes rather than single calls. If you see a dip, consider a check-in.

## Sharing and Privacy

Mood details are shared only at Tier 2 or higher. Your loved one can change this at any time.
