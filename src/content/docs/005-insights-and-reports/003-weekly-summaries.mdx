Weekly summaries give you a simple recap by email. They help you stay informed at a glance.

## What’s Included

- Number of calls and total time
- Mood trend for the week
- Conversation highlights
- Any wellness alerts

## How to Turn It On

1. Go to **Line Settings > Insights & Notifications**
2. Turn on **Weekly Summary**
3. Choose the day and time

## Who Receives It

Summaries go to the email on your account. They follow your sharing settings.
