Conversation highlights give you a quick sense of what stood out. They are short summaries, not transcripts.

## What You’ll See

- Key topics your loved one mentioned
- Notable moments or updates
- Follow-ups you might want to ask about

## No Transcripts

Ultaura does not provide call transcripts in insights. You get short, respectful summaries instead.

## Where to Find Highlights

Go to **Lines > [Line] > Insights** and look for **Conversation Highlights**.

## Sharing and Privacy

Highlights are shared at Tier 3 or higher. Your loved one can change this during any call.
