A line is a phone profile for one person. It holds your loved one’s number, time zone, and preferences.

Line limits depend on your plan. You can have 1 to 4 lines, or unlimited lines on Usage Based.

**Works on any phone, including landlines.**

Schedules follow each line’s time zone. Reminder times are exact and do not move.

In this section, you’ll learn how to:

- Add and verify a new line
- Adjust line settings and quiet hours
- Choose the best voice
- Run a preview test call to the line or a one-time alternate number
- Use vacation mode when your loved one travels
