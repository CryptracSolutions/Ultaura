Add a line when you want Ultaura to call another loved one or a second number.

Ultaura currently supports U.S. phone numbers only.

**Works on any phone, including landlines.**

## Line Limits by Plan

- Free Trial: 1 line
- Care: 1 line
- Comfort: 2 lines
- Family: 4 lines
- Usage Based: Unlimited lines

## Step-by-Step

1. Go to **Lines** in your dashboard.
2. Click **Add Line**.
3. Enter your loved one’s name and phone number.
4. Choose their **time zone**. Schedules follow this time zone.
5. Choose a voice.
6. Optional: Add topics they enjoy and topics to avoid.
7. Verify the phone number.

You can save up to five topics they enjoy. This helps Ultaura start friendly conversations.

Next, complete [Phone Verification](/docs/managing-lines/phone-verification).
