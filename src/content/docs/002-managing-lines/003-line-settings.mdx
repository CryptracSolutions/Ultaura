Line settings let you tailor each line for your loved one.

Go to **Dashboard → Lines → [Line Name] → Settings**.

## Calling & Availability

- **Language:** Sets the conversation language
- **Time zone:** Used for call schedules and quiet hours
- **Quiet hours:** Times when calls are not made (default 9 PM – 9 AM)
- **Voicemail:** What happens if a call goes to voicemail
- **Inbound calls:** Lets your loved one call Ultaura anytime
- **Voice controls:** Lets your loved one manage reminders and schedules by voice
- **Vacation:** Pauses calls and reminders for a date range

**Language options:**
- Auto-detect
- English
- Spanish (Español)
- French (Français)
- German (Deutsch)
- Italian (Italiano)
- Portuguese (Português)
- Japanese
- Korean
- Chinese

Schedules follow the line’s time zone. Reminder times are exact.

**Voicemail options:**
- None: Hang up silently
- Brief: Leave a short message
- Detailed: Include the reason for the call

Learn more about pausing calls in [Vacation Mode](/docs/managing-lines/vacation-mode).

## Insights & Notifications

- **Insights & privacy:** Controls what details appear in insights
- **Weekly summary:** Email recap of calls and mood
- **Missed call alerts:** Email after repeated missed calls

Sharing tiers control how much detail is shown:

- Tier 1: Basic Updates & Safety
- Tier 2: Wellness Check
- Tier 3: Full Summary
- Tier 4: Complete Visibility

Topics appear in insights only at Tier 3 or Tier 4. Learn more in [Sharing with Family](/docs/insights-and-reports/sharing-with-family).

If you share insights with family, you can invite up to five recipients.

Trusted contacts are notified only for high-tier safety events. See [Trusted Contacts](/docs/safety-and-contacts/trusted-contacts).

## Accessibility

Accessibility settings adjust audio clarity and pacing. See [Accessibility](/docs/personalization/accessibility).

## Deleting a Line

From the line detail page, choose **Delete Line** to remove it permanently.
