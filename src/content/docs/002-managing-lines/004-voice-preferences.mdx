Ultaura offers several warm, natural voices. Choose the one your loved one will enjoy most.

## Available Voices

- **Ara:** Warm, gentle, and nurturing
- **Rex:** Clear, confident, and easy to understand
- **Sal:** Relaxed, natural, and conversational
- **Eve:** Bright, cheerful, and upbeat
- **Leo:** Calm, steady, and thoughtful

**Default voice:** Ara.

You select the voice when you add a line. See [Adding a Line](/docs/managing-lines/adding-a-line).

You can preview voices on the homepage with the voice demo.

## Tips

- Pick a voice your loved one feels comfortable with.
- If hearing is a concern, Rex is the clearest voice.
- A test call can help you decide.
