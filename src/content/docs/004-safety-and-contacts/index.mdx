Safety matters. Ultaura looks for signs of concern during calls and keeps you informed. You stay in control of who gets notified.

This section covers the tools that help you respond quickly and thoughtfully. It also explains what Ultaura can and cannot do.

In this section, you’ll learn:

- How trusted contacts get notified by SMS
- When wellness alerts appear and what they mean
- How safety monitoring works and what to do next
