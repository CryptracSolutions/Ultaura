Trusted contacts are people you want to notify if Ultaura detects a serious concern. This gives you a safety net beyond your own alerts.

## How Trusted Contacts Work

Trusted contacts receive SMS alerts only for high-tier safety events. These are significant concerns that need attention.

## Add a Trusted Contact

1. Go to **Line > Contacts**
2. Click **Add Contact**
3. Enter a name and phone number
4. Add a relationship (optional)
5. Acknowledge the consent notice
6. Click **Save**

## What They Receive

- SMS alerts only
- Alerts only for high-tier safety events
- A brief summary of the concern

## Manage Contacts

You can view, edit, or remove contacts at any time. Add more than one if you want coverage.
