Wellness alerts help you notice changes early. They are short, clear updates based on recent calls.

## Alert Levels

- **Info:** General update. A helpful heads-up.
- **Warning:** Moderate concern. You may want to check in.
- **Urgent:** Significant concern. Time-sensitive attention.

## What Triggers an Alert

- Health mentions
- Mood drop compared to recent calls
- Cognitive concerns
- Missed calls over a set threshold

## Where You See Alerts

- Email notifications
- Alerts in your dashboard

## Acknowledge an Alert

After you review an alert, mark it as reviewed. This keeps your alert list clear.

## Privacy Note

Alert details follow your sharing settings. Your loved one’s preferences still apply.
