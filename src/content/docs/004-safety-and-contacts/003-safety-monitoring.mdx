Ultaura listens for signs of distress during conversations. It then responds based on the level of concern.

## How Safety Monitoring Works

- Ultaura listens for concerning language
- Concerns are grouped by severity
- Actions are taken based on that level

## Response Levels

- **Low:** Mild concern. Ultaura offers support and logs it.
- **Medium:** Moderate concern. Ultaura provides resources and logs the event.
- **High:** Significant concern. Ultaura shares crisis resources, alerts family recipients based on their alert settings, and can notify trusted contacts for urgent SMS safety outreach.

## Crisis Resources

Ultaura can share the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline during a call. If there is immediate danger, call 911.

## Important Limits

Ultaura is not an emergency response service. It is not a medical device. It does not replace human care.

## Your Role

Review alerts, check in with your loved one, and keep both family recipients and trusted contacts up to date when needed.
