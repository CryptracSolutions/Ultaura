Sometimes you need to change a single call. You can skip it, snooze it, or reschedule it.

## Skip vs. Snooze vs. Reschedule

- **Skip:** Cancels the next scheduled call. Use it for a one-time conflict.
- **Snooze:** Sets a new time for the call. Use it when you are running late.
- **Reschedule:** Moves one call to a new day or time. Use it for a one-time change.

## Skip the Next Call

1. Open the line’s **Schedule**
2. Click **Skip Next Call**
3. The following scheduled call will proceed as usual

## Snooze a Call

Snooze sets a new time for the next call. If the time is earlier than now, Ultaura schedules it for tomorrow.

## Reschedule One Call

1. Open the line’s **Schedule**
2. Choose **Reschedule**
3. Pick a new date and time

This changes only that one call.

## Voice Requests

If **Voice Controls** are enabled, your loved one can request a skip, snooze, or reschedule during a call.
