Reminders let Ultaura deliver short messages at specific times. They are separate from call schedules and are delivered on their own schedule.

## What Reminders Are

Reminders are scheduled messages. Ultaura calls at the time you set and shares the reminder with your loved one.

## Create a Reminder

1. Go to **Lines** and select your loved one
2. Open **Reminders**
3. Enter the reminder message
4. Choose the date and time
5. Set a recurrence (optional)
6. Save the reminder

## Recurrence Options

You can set reminders as:

- One-time
- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Custom recurring patterns

## Manage Reminders

- **Pause:** Temporarily stops the reminder
- **Snooze:** Delays this reminder (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or tomorrow)
- **Skip Next:** Skips the next scheduled reminder
- **Cancel:** Ends the reminder completely

Your loved one controls what they share about reminders during calls.
