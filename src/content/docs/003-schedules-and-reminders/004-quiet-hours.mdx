Quiet hours help you protect rest time. Ultaura will not place scheduled calls during those hours.

## What Quiet Hours Do

Quiet hours block scheduled calls. They follow the line timezone for your loved one.

## Default Quiet Hours

The default is **9:00 PM to 9:00 AM**. You can change this at any time.

## Set Quiet Hours

1. Go to **Lines** and select your loved one
2. Open **Settings**
3. Find **Quiet Hours**
4. Set the start and end times
5. Save your changes

## How It Works

- Calls during quiet hours are skipped
- Missed calls do not queue up
- The next call happens at the next scheduled time outside quiet hours

## Tips

- Match quiet hours to your loved one’s sleep schedule
- Add daytime quiet hours for naps if needed
