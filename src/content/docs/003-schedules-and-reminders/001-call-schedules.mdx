Call schedules help Ultaura call your loved one at regular times. You choose the days and times that fit their routine.

## What a Schedule Does

A schedule creates recurring check-in calls. It follows the line timezone you set in Line Settings.

Works on any phone, including landlines.

## Create a Schedule

1. Go to **Lines** and select your loved one
2. Open **Schedule**
3. Choose the days of the week
4. Pick a time of day
5. Save the schedule

## Add More Than One Schedule

You can add multiple schedules. This is helpful for morning and evening check-ins.

## Retry Behavior

If a call is not answered, Ultaura retries twice within 30 minutes. This is automatic and cannot be customized.

## When Calls Won’t Be Made

Calls are skipped if:

- Quiet hours are active
- Vacation mode is on
- Your loved one has opted out of calls
